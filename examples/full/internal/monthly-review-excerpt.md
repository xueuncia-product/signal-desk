# Internal corpus example

Drop your own material here — monthly reviews, meeting notes, strategy docs,
anything your organisation writes about its own beat. Nothing in this folder is
ever collected, published or sent anywhere; it is read locally so the gap scan
can compare "what we talk about" against "what our sources return".

Files are split on headings, because one long document would otherwise count as
a single source for every term in it.

## Merchant lifecycle

Activation of newly onboarded merchants remains the weak step. KYB rejections
concentrate on address verification. We should track how BrandB handles KYB
resubmission, since their onboarding funnel appears shorter.

## E-invoicing readiness

Tax authority e-invoicing becomes mandatory for larger merchants next quarter.
Our merchant OS does not yet emit compliant invoice records. Competitors have
not announced anything either, which is itself worth watching.

## Terminal fleet economics

Idle terminals now exceed the threshold we set for the quarter. Swap policy and
firmware rollout cadence both need review; failed transaction reversal rates on
the older device model are the suspected cause.

## Agent channel

Commission structure changes at BrandC pulled several multi-terminal agents
away. Retention offers are being drafted. Watch for a public pricing
announcement rather than relying on second-hand reports from the field.
